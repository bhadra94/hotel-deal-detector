# Hotel Deal Detector 🏨

A free tool that helps hotel investors quickly evaluate potential deals using the professional "4x Revenue Rule."

**Live Tool: [https://bhadra94.github.io/hotel-deal-detector/](https://bhadra94.github.io/hotel-deal-detector/)**

## What It Does

In just 2 minutes, the Hotel Deal Detector tells you if a hotel property is:
- ✅ A potential deal worth investigating
- ❌ Overpriced - walk away
- ⚠️ Borderline - proceed with caution

## How It Works

The tool uses a proven investment formula:
1. Calculates annual room revenue
2. Applies the 4x revenue multiplier
3. Compares against asking price
4. Evaluates income-based value using cap rates

## Who It's For

- First-time hotel investors
- Experienced investors who want quick screening
- Brokers evaluating listings
- Anyone considering hotel investments

## The Math Behind It

The calculator implements the "2-minute drill" used by professional hotel investors:
- **Max Purchase Price** = (Annual Room Revenue × 4) - Renovation Budget
- **Income Value** = Net Operating Income ÷ Cap Rate
